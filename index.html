Who We Are

Loxo Tour and Travel is a Rwanda-based tour and travel company dedicated to providing memorable, safe, and personalized travel experiences for local and international visitors in Kigali and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a trusted and leading tour and travel company in Rwanda and East Africa, creating memorable travel experiences through exceptional service, innovation, and sustainable tourism.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, safe, and personalized travel services while promoting Rwanda's natural beauty, wildlife, and cultural heritage to the world.

Our Services

Gorilla Trekking & Wildlife Safaris

Excursions to Volcanoes and Akagera National Parks for gorilla tracking and wildlife safaris.

Language Translation & Guidance

Professional language translation (Kinyarwanda, English, French) and personalized tour guidance.

Cultural Tours & City Excursions

Authentic cultural heritage tours and guided city sightseeing around vibrant Kigali.

Transfers & Reservations

Airport transfers, transport logistics, hotel reservations, and customized holiday packages.
